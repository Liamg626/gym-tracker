## What this is

This is the list of **Exercises** Liam does, or might do, at his gym. It feeds the "EXL: Generate the library" story, which researches each Exercise's **Muscles** and writes the **Exercise Library** JSON file (ADR-0006). Muscle tagging is not done here. Each family sits under its main **Muscle Group** only as a rough guide.

How the tags work:

- **current**: one of the 29 exercises Liam named. He named families, not exact variants, so `current` is on the family heading (e.g. "Lateral Raise — current").
- **option**: every variant row, and every addition. Additions are the 3 most common exercises per Muscle Group that are not already on Liam's list. Their family headings say "option (addition)".
- Exact variants are not ticked here: Liam's Routines in the app show which ones he actually does (Liam, 2026-10-17). Every entry goes into the library.

Every row names one specific variant. Names are unique across the whole file.

Items marked **(assumed)** are equipment or machines not confirmed in Liam's gym description. Check them before they go in the library.

## Assumptions and open questions

- **Equipment.** Everything marked "(assumed)" was not in Liam's description. The pull-up bar is the biggest one: Pull-Up, Hanging Leg Raise and Hanging Knee Raise need it.
- **Leg curl.** Liam said "leg extension/curl" without saying seated or lying. Both are listed; the lying rows are marked assumed.
- **Group placement.** Dip is under Chest (triceps noted). Back Extension is under Back (erector spinae). Deadlift is under Back. Step-Up is under Glutes and hips. Bulgarian Split Squat, Lunge and the squats are under Quads, though they work the glutes a lot. The "Generate the library" story will tag the real Muscles.
- **Family edges.** Stiff-Leg Deadlift is a variant of Romanian Deadlift, not an addition. Glute Bridge is a variant of Hip Thrust. Straight-Arm Cable Pulldown is a variant of Lat Pulldown. Machine Ab Crunch sits under Cable Crunch. Floor crunches and sit-ups are an addition family, since Liam named only the cable crunch.
- **Weak commonness evidence.** No good usage data was found per Muscle Group for core, glutes, hamstrings or calves beyond the sources above. Additions marked "weaker evidence" were picked from exercise directories and judgement. Calves and shins has only one addition (Tibialis Raise), because the common calf exercises are all variants of Liam's calf raise family.
- **Less standard variants.** Cable Single-Arm Bayesian Hammer Curl, Smith Machine Skull Crusher, Barbell Seal Row, B-Stance Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift and Dumbbell Lying Leg Curl are real but less common. Smith Machine Skull Crusher and Seal Row are in the StrengthLog directory; the others were not checked against a library.
- **Lean-forward hip abduction** and the leg press foot positions are listed as separate entries because they change the target muscles. They could be merged with their base exercise if Liam prefers fewer entries.
