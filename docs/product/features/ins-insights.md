# INS: Charts and the Weekly Breakdown

Full charts work best on the wider dashboard; the phone gets compact versions. Charts use Recharts.

## Stories

**INS: Strength chart per Exercise** (v1.1): Estimated 1RM over time, with PRs marked

**INS: Body charts** (v1.1): Trend Weight with the raw Weigh-ins faded behind it; Body Fat Estimate; one line per tape measurement

**INS: Workout calendar** (v1.1): month view of the days with a Workout

**INS: Goals view** (v1.1): all Milestones and Body Goals with progress

**INS: Volume per Muscle** (v1.2): weekly Volume per Muscle (rolled up into groups for readability), and Working Sets per Muscle against the target ranges from Settings

**INS: Weekly Breakdown** (v1.2)
- Monday–Sunday (Europe/London), available from Monday, shown in the app and on the dashboard, with past weeks browsable
- Contains: Workouts vs Weekly Target and total duration; Volume per Muscle group with change since last week; Working Sets per Muscle vs target range; new PRs; change in Trend Weight and Body Fat Estimate; Watch Data averages (resting HR, HRV, sleep) once WCH exists; progress on each goal
- No AI-written summary (decided)
